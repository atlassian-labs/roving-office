What this is

An office full of characters, and the characters are your AI agents.

The Roving Office is a soft-3D isometric office. The characters in it are agent sessions — Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Rovo CLI, OpenClaw — one character per conversation. When an agent starts a piece of work somebody walks in and sits down at a desk. When it looks something up, they get up and go to the bookshelf. When it finishes, they carry the work to the mailbox and post it. When it fails, they crumple it up and bin it.

That is the entire idea. The room is the status board, and nothing in it is a metaphor you have to decode.

One room, and everybody you can see is a real conversation somewhere.

See it now → the demo office

Nothing to install. That room runs on simulated agents, so it is already busy — people arriving, coffee breaks, parcels in the mailbox, somebody stooped over the telescope. Reception will cut you an empty office of your own.

It is not a fleet monitor and does not want to be. It holds about a dozen desks, and it is a way to watch a team work. It is also, deliberately, a bit silly: the coffee machine is a three-group espresso machine with cups warming on top, and one print in six overshoots the tray and lands on the floor. None of that tells you anything. It is there because an office with nothing to notice is a screensaver.
