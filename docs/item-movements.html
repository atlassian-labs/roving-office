Every object the room is built from, turnable — and the only gallery of them there is. Each is built by the same builder the office uses, so a prop that changes and a page that is reloaded cannot disagree.

The whole room is made of primitives: boxes, cylinders, the odd icosahedron. No models, no textures loaded from disk, nothing downloaded. That is easy to say and hard to picture, which is what this page is for.

Not here: walls, floors, the storeys below, the skyline. Those are arrangements rather than objects — a wall is only a wall in the presence of the room it closes — and they have their own pictures in buildings and themes. The door, the lift and the window are here, because each is a made thing that a wall merely holds. What each object is for, rather than what it is, is the room, prop by prop.
